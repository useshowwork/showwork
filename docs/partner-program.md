# Partner Program behavior

Creators apply from `/dashboard/partners`. Admin approval activates a unique referral link and grants one month of complimentary access. Rejected creators may reapply; suspended creators cannot. New customers must use the referral link during signup and verify their email for attribution to be saved.

New partner approvals require an explicit commission percentage and duration, plus an optional customer discount percentage and duration. Existing partners/referrals retain their original 10% / 12-month agreements with no customer discount. Referral agreements are snapshotted at verified signup. Partners earn their agreed percentage of actual verified LIVE payment revenue for Delivery and Content Workspace initial subscriptions and renewals. Catalogue prices are not read by the commission calculation: discounted annual charges and legacy subscription charges use the amount actually recorded as paid. Test payments, unverified payments, portfolio payments and one-time project payments do not qualify. Commission amounts round down to whole naira.

For a 10% agreement, ₦4,900 paid earns ₦490; ₦55,860 paid annually earns ₦5,586. Annual commission is recorded when the payment occurs, rather than spread across months.

The first qualifying payment starts the referral’s agreed commission eligibility window. Payments at or after its anniversary do not qualify. Leap-day anniversaries clamp to February 28. Stopping new partner referrals does not cancel existing referred customers' eligibility.

Commissions begin pending and require admin approval. Partners need a saved bank account and at least ₦5,000 of approved, unallocated commissions to request a payout. Allocations prevent the same earnings from being withdrawn twice; admin settlement records payment rather than initiating an automatic bank transfer. Voided records are excluded from total earnings.

Duplicate webhook delivery retries missing commission processing. Serializable transactions protect the initial eligibility window against concurrent payments. Payments marked UNVERIFIED after provider outages still require revenue reconciliation before earning commissions; replaying a webhook alone does not reclassify revenue.

Validation: `node --test tests/partner-commissions.test.cjs tests/payment-revenue.test.cjs tests/workspace-pricing.test.cjs tests/calendar-payment-return.test.cjs` and `npx tsc --noEmit --incremental false`. Production checkout, webhook delivery and actual payout settlement require an end-to-end environment check. These local checks do not certify deployment configuration or existing financial records.

Partner customer discounts use private SELECTED-audience billing offers for Delivery and Marketing Workspace. A zero discount creates no offer. Discount duration starts at the first discounted payment for each product, stops after the agreed cycles, and restores standard pricing. Offers do not stack; normal offer selection rules choose one eligible offer. Annual billing requires durations divisible by 12; shorter durations apply to monthly billing only. Commission is calculated from discounted revenue actually paid. Approval form supports whole-number commission 0–100%, 1–120 months; customer discounts 0–99%, 1–36 months (0 months when disabled). The generic fixed-terms PDF is no longer attached to welcome emails; the email and dashboard communicate the approved terms.

Deployment requires migration `20261106190000_partner_agreement_terms` before running the new application/worker code, then Prisma Client generation. No migration was applied by the implementation task.

## Financial controls (6 November 2026)

- PAID payouts are terminal. Pending and approved **unpaid** requests may be cancelled, releasing allocations; paid allocations remain. Admin payout and commission reviews use Serializable transactions and conditional status writes. Conflicts return 409 rather than overwriting another administrator. The old commission endpoint delegates to the canonical endpoint.
- Payout accounts are selected from Paystack’s paginated Nigerian bank directory and resolved server-side. Account holder names are supplied by Paystack, not trusted from the browser. Each payout snapshots its verified bank destination. Legacy accounts must be reverified; legacy payout destinations remain explicitly unknown rather than backfilled from current details. Cancel and recreate old unverified pending/approved requests before settlement.
- Signed refund/dispute webhooks re-fetch the issue through Paystack before recording it. Open/uncertain issues hold payouts. Processed refunds and accepted disputes proportionally reduce unpaid commissions, rounded up for the reversed portion so net payable cannot exceed earned commission. Related dispute refunds are deduplicated; independent refunds accumulate, capped at the original commission. Confirmed loss cannot be silently overwritten by an older provider status. Issues arriving before a commission are retained and applied when payment processing creates/replays it.
- A payment change cancels pending/approved payout allocations in the same Serializable transaction. Paid payouts/commissions are not rewritten; their reversals create an outstanding recovery amount and block new payouts until reconciled. Admin → Partners → Payment reviews & recoveries supports provider rechecks and recording funds actually received with a reference. Declined disputes remain held until an administrator records confirmed bank clearance; only provider-resolved declined disputes can receive this clearance. These actions do not initiate refunds, transfers, or automatic debits from partners.
- Audit events record administrative actors and financial/lifecycle actions, including bank verification (only bank code and last four digits in the audit detail). Bank resolution verifies the destination/account name; it is not identity verification of the person controlling the Showwork account.
- Referral codes persist in an HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, 30-day cookie. Last explicit well-formed referral link wins; server-side ACTIVE/isActive validation is required at signup and OTP verification. Login/signup switches retain the code and product/plan return destination. A cookie never bypasses eligibility checks.
- Pause referrals stops new attribution but allows previously earned payouts. Suspend also blocks payout processing. Resume restores access without granting a second welcome benefit. Existing customer eligibility and commission histories remain intact. Expired referral counts are derived from commissionEndsAt, so stale stored ACTIVE labels cannot inflate reporting.
- Admin total commissions exclude VOIDED and reversed amounts. Total payouts includes PAID only. Historical paid amounts remain gross disbursements; recovery amounts are separate. Partner request eligibility includes bank verification, active requests, payment holds and recovery debt, matching the API’s guards.

### Deployment and operations

Apply `20261106190000_partner_agreement_terms` and `20261106200000_partner_financial_controls` before deploying the corresponding app/worker code, then generate Prisma Client and restart the app and billing/publishing workers. Neither migration was applied during implementation. Confirm Paystack webhook subscriptions deliver `refund.*` and `charge.dispute.*` events to the existing signature-verified webhook. Bank resolution and issue verification require the existing valid PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY and provider connectivity. Failed verification never saves an unverified account; failed issue verification returns 503 for replay.
